ClipHardAF

A personal desktop tool — not a public service.

ClipHardAF runs on its owner's computer. It cuts the owner's podcast videos into short vertical clips and, with the owner's consent, uploads chosen clips as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API). The owner reviews each draft and publishes it from the TikTok app.

It is used only by its owner, on accounts the owner controls.
