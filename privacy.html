Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

ClipHardAF is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own accounts.

Data accessed

  • TikTok basic profile (user.info.basic): the account's identifier and display name, to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): to send videos the owner selects to the owner's TikTok drafts.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the tool's local configuration. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deletion

Disconnecting the account in the tool deletes its tokens; access can also be revoked from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
